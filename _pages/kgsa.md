I maintain a mailing list of Korean graduate students at Georgetown University's Main Campus.
If you're a grad student not affiliated with the Law School, SFS, or the MBA program, we'd love to have you join us!

To join or to disseminate information, please contact me at my email address.

In the past year, Korean Grads @ GU has hosted a WBC watch party and an end-of-semester hangout.
We plan to expand our footprint in the coming semesters.
